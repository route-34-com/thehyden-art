1 · Hero

You paint the wall

The site opens on an empty room. Move the cursor or drag a finger, and Nabhi Kamalam appears in brushstrokes wherever you paint. After about a third of the wall, the rest fills in by itself and the headline lands. If nobody touches it, a ghost brush paints a stroke to show the way.

Sells: hand-painted, felt instead of read.

2 · Founder section or Projects

From the drawing board to the wall

The room starts as an architect's elevation: lines draw in with dimension marks and a title block. Then the real finished room washes in over the drawing. Drag the slider to scrub. The linework is traced from her own photo by edge detection, no AI.

Sells: designed by an architect, not just a seller.

3 · Sales tool

See it on your wall, live

On a phone, tap Camera, point at your wall and drag the four corners onto its edges. The mural sits on your actual wall in perspective, blended with your room's light. No app. On a laptop, use a photo or the demo room.

Sells: removes the "will it suit my room?" doubt. The strongest enquiry driver.

Drag the four dots onto the corners of your wall.

The camera needs HTTPS on a phone (fine on the live domain). Instagram's in-app browser may block it, so the real version would show a short "open in browser" prompt there.

4 · Murals section opener

Step into the mural

The painting is split into depth layers behind a carved jharokha arch: the mural at the back, nandi and lamps in the middle, the parrot and lotus up front. Layers move at different speeds with the mouse, or with the phone's tilt. The real version would split one of her murals into layers by hand, with no AI.

Sells: her art as a world, not a product photo.

5 · Enquiry

A mural sized to your wall

Type your wall's size. Thota recomposes live: the two trees hold the edges, and the palm grove spreads out or repeats as the wall grows, so nothing is just stretched or cropped. A 5'6" figure shows the scale, the way an architect draws it. Then one tap sends the size to Mrudhula.

Sells: made to measure, by an architect.
