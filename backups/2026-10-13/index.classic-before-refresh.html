Meet the founder

Hi, I'm Mrudhula.

“Thehyden.art isn’t just about artworks and wallpapers; it’s about storytelling, connection, and the celebration of timeless beauty.”

An architect by training, Mrudhula started The Hyden Art out of a deep love for blending tradition with contemporary aesthetics. What began as a simple wish to capture the beauty of Indian heritage has grown into a studio making wallpapers and art frames that sit easily in modern homes while honouring their cultural roots.

Can I get a design in my own size and colours?

Yes. Every wallpaper and mural is made to order for your exact wall measurements, and most designs can be recoloured to suit your room.

How do I measure my wall?

Measure the full width and height in feet or metres, skirting to ceiling. Send us a photo of the wall too, and we'll double-check the measurements and plan around switches, windows and doors.

What material is the wallpaper printed on?

We'll recommend the right finish for your room. Ask us about the options for bedrooms, living rooms, kids' rooms and humid spaces.

Do you install it?

Our team handles installation in select cities. Elsewhere we ship it ready to hang, with instructions any local wallpaper installer can follow.

How long does it take?

Most orders are designed, printed and dispatched within 2–3 weeks of confirming the design. Custom artwork can take a little longer.

Do you ship outside India?

Yes, we ship worldwide. Shipping costs and delivery time depend on your country, so we'll include them in your quote.

Can you create a completely custom mural?

Absolutely. Tell us the story, the room and the mood, and we'll paint something that exists only on your wall.
