The studio

thehyden.art is led by Mrudhula, an architect, artist and designer.

“thehyden.art isn’t just about artworks and wallpapers; it’s about storytelling, connection, and the celebration of timeless beauty.”

An architect by training, Mrudhula started thehyden.art out of a deep love for blending tradition with contemporary aesthetics. What began as a simple wish to capture the beauty of Indian heritage has grown into a studio making wallpapers and art frames that sit easily in modern homes while honouring their cultural roots.
